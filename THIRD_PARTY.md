# Third-party content

assets/spotify-logo.png is the existing repository's official green Spotify logo,
preserved without modification. Spotify owns this mark. Use remains subject to its
[design and branding guidelines](https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/design);
it is not covered by any future license for this project's source code.

Spotify supplies playlist, track and album metadata and album artwork at runtime.
Those assets are never bundled with this rewrite. They remain subject to Spotify's
terms and the rights of their creators.

Pillow's bundled default font is supplied by the Pillow dependency. No font files
are bundled with this repository. See [Pillow's font documentation](https://pillow.readthedocs.io/en/stable/reference/ImageFont.html)
and the dependency's own license.

The demo's labels and colored placeholder tiles are synthetic. They contain no
Spotify listening data or downloaded album artwork.
